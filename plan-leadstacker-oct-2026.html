01

What we found

Your brief asks for CPL, CPA, ROAS and lead quality at the same time, across three platforms, feeding a call center and GHL. The gap that usually sits between those goals is simple: the ad platforms only see the form fill, while the money is made on the qualified call. Fix that signal and scaling gets far safer.

1. Platforms optimize toward the cheapest lead, not the best one

High impact

When Meta, TikTok and Google only receive a lead event, each algorithm finds people who fill forms cheaply. CPL looks fine, the call center sees low contact and low intent, and the buyer or offer partner pushes back on quality.

Signal today (typical)

  • Lead event fires on form submit
  • Same weight for junk and qualified leads
  • Call outcome stays inside GHL
  • Scaling raises volume and lowers quality

Signal after week 1

  • Lead, contacted, qualified, sold stages tracked
  • Qualified and sold sent back to each platform
  • Bidding learns from call center outcomes
  • Scaling raises volume at a held quality bar

2. GHL and call outcomes are the missing attribution layer

High impact

The data to fix this already exists in GHL. It needs to flow back to the platforms through server side events, with click IDs captured on every lead so outcomes match to the ad that produced them.

3. Testing needs a fixed cadence and kill rules

Medium impact

Audiences, creatives, offers and landing pages all need testing, but testing four things at once on three platforms burns budget without a clear read. A fixed weekly cadence with written kill and scale rules keeps tests clean and decisions fast.
